Changelog

Every release gets a new version number. The current build is shown on the download page.

1.8 current

  • Trigger reverted to the earlier behaviour (small corner window, 44 dp threshold, tap opens); only the diagonal-angle check stays fixed.
  • Adjustment mode (long-press the middle of the orbit): move the whole fan — handy on tablets — pull a ring in or out to change the spacing, resize icons, and drag an app onto another ring to pin it.
  • Smoother edge fade: icons are drawn one step beyond the arc and fade, shrink and sink on a smoothstep curve.
  • English UI with every permission explained in plain language.
  • Privacy Policy and this changelog published on the site.

1.7

  • In-app updates: checks version.json on every launch, downloads the APK, verifies SHA-256 and opens the installer.
  • Thumb spotlight: the icon under your finger grows, outer rings step aside.
  • Moon-set fade for icons leaving the arc.

1.6

  • Release plumbing: signing, publishSite, container deployment driven by the version file.

1.5

  • The orbit slides out from the thumb corner and slides back when closed.
  • Larger gesture area; diagonal-angle check fixed for tall screens.

1.4

  • Folders in the middle ring, expanded on the outer ring.
  • Long-press panel with icon size and ring spacing sliders.
  • Bigger default icons, more space between rings.

1.3

  • Permission diagnostics plus explanations for restricted settings and Advanced Protection.

1.2

  • In-app help card when the overlay permission is denied.

1.1

  • First release: diagonal gesture from the bottom-right corner, three rings, roulette spin, alphabetical order, thumb calibration, overlay and accessibility triggers.
